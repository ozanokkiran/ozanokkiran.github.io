What is a Slope Field?

A differential equation tells us the slope (gradient) of a function at any given point (x, y). The small arrows represent these slopes.

Try it: Click anywhere on the grid! You will drop an "initial condition" point $(x_0, y_0)$ into the field.

The pink line is the Particular Solution, computed using the Runge-Kutta (RK4) algorithm to follow the "current" of the slope field perfectly.
