The Drop Zone (Randomness) Imagine a square board with a circle perfectly fitted inside it. We drop dots completely randomly across the entire square (not aiming for the circle). Any point has an equal chance of landing anywhere.
The Mathematical Correlation Why does this find Pi?
Area of Circle = π × r²
Area of Square = (2r)² = 4r²
Ratio = (π × r²) / (4r²) = π / 4
π ≈ 4 × (Inside / Total)
