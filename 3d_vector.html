Vector Operations

Addition (u + v): Places the tail of vector v at the head of vector u. The result is the diagonal of the parallelogram formed by u and v.

Cross Product (u × v): Produces a new vector that is strictly perpendicular (orthogonal) to both u and v. The direction follows the Right-Hand Rule.
