The Head-to-Tail Method

To add vectors geometrically, place the tail of the second vector (v) at the head (tip) of the first vector (u).

The Resultant vector (w) is drawn from the tail of the first to the head of the second.

Mathematically:
w = (ux + vx, uy + vy)

Resultant: w = u + v = (4, -1)
