Where does the curve come from?

The curve isn't just a random shape. It represents a Distribution of Data. When you measure things in nature (like heights of people or test scores), most values clump around the average (the peak), and extreme values are rare (the tails). The curve is the "ideal blanket" covering that data pile.

Mean (μ) & Standard Deviation (σ)

μ (Mean): The center of gravity. It shifts the entire curve left or right.
σ (Std Dev): The "spread". A small σ means data is tightly packed (tall/skinny curve). A large σ means data is widely spread out (short/wide curve).
