Why does a polynomial look like this?

A Taylor series allows us to approximate any smooth function using simple polynomials (x, x2, x3...). By matching the function's value and its derivatives at x=0, the polynomial perfectly mimics the curve.

As you increase the Number of Terms (N), the approximation perfectly hugs the original curve further away from the center.
