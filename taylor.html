Why does a polynomial look like a wave?

A Taylor series allows us to approximate any smooth function (like sine) using simple polynomials ($x, x^2, x^3...$). By matching the function's value and all its derivatives at a single point (here, $x=0$), the polynomial "learns" the curve's behavior.

As you increase the Number of Terms (N), the approximation perfectly hugs the original curve further and further away from the center.
