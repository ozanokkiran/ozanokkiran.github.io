Why do they form a polygon?

By De Moivre's Theorem, the equation $z^n = 1$ is rewritten as $z^n = e^{i(2\pi k)}$. Taking the $n$-th root divides the angle by $n$. This forces all $n$ roots to lie on the unit circle (radius = 1), perfectly spaced by an angle of $2\pi/n$ radians ($360°/n$), creating a regular polygon.
